# ASD-STE100 not-approved word index

Lookup companion to `simplified-technical-english.md`. Machine-extracted from the Part 2 dictionary of *ASD-STE100 Issue 9 (2025)*: every word the dictionary lists as **not approved**, with its approved alternative.

Left column is the word you must not use. Right column is what to write instead, with the alternative's part of speech. `(TN)` means the alternative is a *technical noun* and `(TV)` a *technical verb* -- meaning the fix is usually a sentence rewrite that uses the word as a noun or verb respectively, not a drop-in swap (for example `corrode (v)` -> `CORROSION (TN)` means "Corrosion occurs on the surface," not "The surface corrodes").

Caveats: extracted from the PDF's table layout, so a small number of rows may be truncated or mis-split. The dictionary's own examples are the authority. This index does **not** include the 875 approved words with their approved meanings -- for those, and for the STE / non-STE example pairs that show correct usage, consult the PDF at `~/Downloads/ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English - Issue 9 (2025).pdf`.
